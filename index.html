Built and running

Printed and running on an Anet ET4: the 4010 blowers on the original ducts, the 2510 hotend fan on the front clamp and the LEDs lighting the nozzle.

What this remix adds

  • 4010 adapter to fit the Ender 3's stock 4010 blowers to the original 5015 ducts, in two versions: normal (the blower in the original position, held by its mounting screw) and flat (the blower on top with its intake facing the front, like the hotend fan; the adapter is only 15 mm tall, so the blowers sit low).
  • LED mount that fits on the inside of each original duct, held with a zip tie, and aims two LEDs of a 24 V LED strip with one lensed LED per segment, like the light strips sold for the Bambu Lab A1, at the nozzle.
  • One-piece duct with the 4010 adapter and the LED channel built in, in both versions, if you prefer to reprint the ducts.
  • 25 → 30 mm fan adapter so the 2510 hotend fan of an Anycubic Kobra X fits Locos3D's 30 mm fan clamp.

Assembly notes

  • LEDs: on this kind of strip each LED is its own segment. Cut it at the marks, two LEDs per side, and wire both pieces in parallel to 24 V.
  • 2510 fan adapter: it is held by the clamp's own two M3 screws, which need to be about 4 mm longer, and the fan is fixed with 4 self-tapping M2.5 screws.
