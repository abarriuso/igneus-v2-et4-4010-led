LEDs wired in parallel

Each strip segment carries its own resistor and runs straight from 24 V. Wired in parallel, all four LEDs get the same voltage, so they switch on together at the same brightness, and if one fails the others stay lit.

Blowers wired in parallel

Both 4010 blowers hang off the printer's part-cooling fan output. In parallel they get the same voltage and the same PWM, so they start together and always run at the same speed on both sides of the nozzle.

4010 right 4010 left 2510 hotend

Printed parts in black. The mod itself is the 4010 adapter, the LED mount and the 2510 fan adapter; the rest is the original Igneus V2. The fans are approximate models of the Ender 3 stock 4010 blower and the Kobra X 2510.
